How this model works

Three color signals come out of the captured region — the CIELAB a* channel (the red–green axis), HSV saturation, and a redness ratio — and run through a logistic model, the same baseline class published anemia-detection research compares against deep learning. The weights here are provisional starting values, not fitted to patient data yet.

To make it real: CP-AnemiC is a public dataset of 710 annotated conjunctival images from children in Ghana with lab-confirmed hemoglobin levels, built for exactly this task. A 2025 study quantized a MobileNet model trained on it down to FP16 for edge deployment and held over 92% accuracy — proof this class of model runs on-device, not just in a lab.
